Methodology and sources

The catalog shows what happened, measured the same way for every kind of investment, with a source and a date next to each number. It does not give advice or forecasts.

Not investment advice. Past returns do not predict future ones. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell. Rankings and liquidity ratings never depend on whether a platform pays us.

Where numbers come from

LevelWhat it meansExamples
market priceDaily prices of something you can actually buy, from an exchange feed.ETFs, stocks, coins, currencies, listed clubs
indexA published index built by a statistics body or an industry provider.BIS house prices, NCREIF, Liv-ex, Cambridge Associates
reportA study, survey or annual report; usually yearly and with a lag.Knight Frank Wealth Report, BizBuySell, academic studies
platform figureReturns stated by the platform that sells the product. Treat with care.Mintos, Fundrise, Royalty Exchange
estimateExpert estimate without a full data set.Typical selling times, some fees

An empty field is shown as “no data”. That is more honest than a made-up figure: some markets (domains, game accounts, athlete income deals) simply have no reliable public statistics.

How metrics are calculated

MetricDefinition
Return, 1 yearChange from the close 365 days ago to the latest close. For funds and shares it uses prices adjusted for dividends, so it is a total return.
Return, 5 and 10 yearsCompound annual growth rate over the period. Shown only if the history really reaches back that far.
SwingAnnualised standard deviation of weekly log returns over the last 3 years (quarterly changes for house price indexes, last 10 years).
Worst dropLargest fall from a previous peak, weekly closes over the last ~10 years (full history for house price indexes), with the dates.
Vs stocksCorrelation of weekly returns with the S&P 500 ETF (SPY) over 3 years.
Volume, 30 daysSum of daily exchange volume × close, in US dollars, for US-dollar listings.
Liquidity 1–55: sells in seconds on an exchange. 4: minutes to days. 3: days to a few weeks. 2: weeks. 1: months or years, or locked.
Group figuresMedian of the ways in the group that have a figure. The number of ways used is shown on hover.
Way headlineWhere a way has a listed proxy (for example a hedge fund replication ETF), the first tracked asset is the headline and the industry index is shown next to it as “report”.
DepositsWorld Bank deposit interest rate and consumer price inflation, latest year per country; real return = rate minus inflation.

House price indexes are nominal and in local currency: rent, maintenance and taxes are not included. Annualised figures from cumulative published changes (for example Knight Frank 5- and 10-year changes) are converted by us and marked so.
